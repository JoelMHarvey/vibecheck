Working

    Rankings, search terms and backlinks aren't in this check. Google Search Console shows those for your own site, free.

    What it checks

    Can a crawler read it?

    Whether your words are in the HTML or only drawn by JavaScript (the blank-page problem most Lovable, Bolt and Vite apps ship with), robots.txt, the sitemap and its dates.

    Can an AI quote it?

    llms.txt, which AI crawlers you let in, structured data for who you are and what you charge, and machine-readable files.

    Does each page say what it is?

    Titles, descriptions, one clear H1, link previews, sections stuck behind #fragments, alt text.

    One address, not three

    www against bare, HTTP against HTTPS, redirect chains, canonical tags, links that go through redirects.

    Would a stranger trust it?

    A way to get in touch, a privacy policy, who's behind it, and what it costs.

    Fix prompts, and how to check

    Every finding says why it matters, gives a prompt for your AI tool, and tells you how to see the fix worked.

    About our crawler

    Requests from this page come from our server with the user agent vibecheck-visibility/1.0 (+https://psychosecurity.io/visibility). Someone typed your address into this page, or ran the command-line tool against it.

    A check reads robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt, your home page and up to 20 more pages, the other form of your domain (www or bare), the http:// address, your link-preview image and the sizes of up to 12 images. At most six requests at a time, and one check per site per minute however many people ask. It never submits forms or follows links off your site, and it obeys robots.txt rules for vibecheck-visibility. To keep it out:

    User-agent: vibecheck-visibility
Disallow: /

    Prefer the command line?

    The same check, from your own machine, with no limits. Python 3.9+, no dependencies. It exits 1 on any high or critical finding, so it can run in CI.

    python3 -m vibecheck --visibility https://myapp.com --markdown report.md
python3 -m vibecheck --visibility https://myapp.com --json
